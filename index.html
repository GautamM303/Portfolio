About me

I'm Gautam Manerikar, a third-year student in Engineering Science at the University of Toronto, majoring in Robotics Engineering.

I got started with LEGO robots in the SciTech program at Tomken Road Middle School and kept going from there. In high school I founded the robotics club at St. Francis Xavier and led mechanical design for our FIRST Robotics team, which finished 5th internationally.

These days most of my work sits between software and hardware: motion planning and computer vision for a robot arm, sensors wired to microcontrollers, and circuits I etch and solder myself. On the aUToronto team I work on the HD map that lets our self-driving car follow its route through the city.

Outside of engineering, baseball has been a big part of my life. I played competitive REP AA baseball through middle and high school, played varsity baseball at St. Francis Xavier from grade 10 to 12 and varsity ping pong in grade 12, and have umpired with the Mississauga North Baseball Association since 2019.
